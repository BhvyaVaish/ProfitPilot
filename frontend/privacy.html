Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 1, 2026 · Engineered by Bhvya Vaish

1. Introduction & Overview

ProfitPilot ("we", "our", or "the Platform"), developed by Bhvya Vaish, is committed to safeguarding the privacy and commercial confidentiality of Indian Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs). This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used, and how your business data remains isolated and protected.

2. Information We Collect

  • Authentication Information: Name, email address, and OAuth authentication tokens via Google Identity / Firebase Authentication.
  • Business & Catalog Data: Product names, categories, selling prices, cost prices, stock counts, and sales history uploaded or created by the user.
  • Invoicing Records: GST invoice details, item lines, timestamps, and customer names (optional) recorded during billing transactions.
  • Technical Data: Browser user-agent and basic performance logs used solely to diagnose errors and prevent platform abuse.

3. Multi-Tenant Data Isolation & Security

Your inventory and sales data is strictly isolated using tenant-level identifiers in Google Cloud Firestore. No shopkeeper can access, query, or view another merchant's stock or pricing data. Security rules enforced directly at the database layer prevent cross-account data leakage.

4. Third-Party Integrations

ProfitPilot integrates trusted infrastructure providers including Google Cloud Platform, Firebase (authentication & real-time database), and Vercel (serverless hosting and edge CDN). We do not sell, rent, or monetize your business records with third-party advertisers.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You retain full ownership of your data at all times. If you wish to export your catalog, you can download it directly from the Inventory module. To request complete deletion of your account and records, please reach out to our contact address below.
